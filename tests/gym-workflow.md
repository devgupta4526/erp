# Gym workflow checks

The browser suite covers creating a plan, registering a member, recording a payment and checking in. Use a disposable PostgreSQL database and separate browser sessions for the extended checks below. Record results in [TEST_RESULTS.md](../docs/TEST_RESULTS.md).

1. **Fresh and existing workspaces.** Apply all migrations on a fresh database. On a separate database at migration 004, create a member, legacy membership and attendance record before applying 005. Confirm all original records remain usable in the workspace and the legacy membership can receive a paid renewal.
2. **Membership plans.** As an organization owner, create a 30-day INR plan priced at 1250.50. Confirm the price in the plan table and payment selector. Try another active plan with the same name in a different case. Confirm rejection. Try zero price, a fractional day count, and an unsupported currency through the service and confirm no plan is inserted.
3. **Registration, payment and attendance.** Register a member from the Gym page. Confirm the member begins inactive. Record a received payment for the plan, confirm one receipt for 125050 minor units, and confirm that membership covers today through 29 days afterward, inclusive. Record attendance twice and confirm there is one check-in in the roster and recent attendance report.
4. **Renewal dates.** Give an active member a new paid term and confirm the next term starts the day after their latest term ends. For an expired member, confirm a paid term starts today in UTC. Confirm a member with an expired term cannot record attendance until a new term covers today.
5. **Duplicate references and concurrent writes.** Submit two payment records with the same receipt reference in one organization, including submissions for different members. Confirm exactly one receipt, membership change and payment audit event commit. Submit concurrent payments for one member with distinct references and confirm both terms are recorded with contiguous dates.
6. **Archive a plan.** Archive an existing plan after receiving a payment. Confirm it disappears from payment options and rejects new payment records while past receipts and membership periods remain available. Confirm another plan can use the archived name.
7. **Authorization and active workspace.** Confirm a member-role user can read the roster and attendance but cannot obtain payment history or write Gym data. Confirm an expired or unentitled organization cannot read or mutate Gym records. Use two workspaces to confirm member IDs, plan IDs and receipts stay scoped. Leave the Gym page open, switch workspaces in another tab, and submit the stale form. Confirm the action does not write to either workspace.
8. **Reporting boundaries and pagination.** Seed attendance 29 and 30 days before the current UTC date. Confirm only the more recent result appears. With more than 25 members, confirm pagination has no duplicate member IDs across stable result pages. With more than 25 receipts, verify the latest 25 are shown to authorized staff.
