## Shared boundaries

Contact references, invoices, payments, branch membership, notifications and audit trails should be shared where the domain semantics align. Hotel room availability, clinical visits, gym memberships and rental bookings need separate lifecycle rules. Pharmacy batches, jewellery vault items and optical catalog inventory have distinct traceability and validation. Clinical and child records need restricted access and a production readiness review.

BusinessOS currently supports member registration, configurable plans, manually recorded receipts that activate or renew memberships, one attendance per UTC date, and daily attendance reporting. Attendance correction, local business time zones, additional analytics, QR check-in, trainer coordination, expenses, classes, reminders and provider payment links remain pending. See the [Gym verification cases](../tests/gym-workflow.md) for renewal and legacy data scenarios.
